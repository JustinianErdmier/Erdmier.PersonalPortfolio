ABOUT ME

Learning Enthusiast

My name is Justinian Erdmier, and I started my journey as a software engineer in 2021. After graduating from Eleven Fifty Academy, I accepted my first professional role at Lieberman Technologies. I have worked with a variety of technologies, including .NET, C#, JavaScript, PHP, and so much more. My second role was with Toyota, where I designed and developed solutions for their manufacturing and administrative processes.

I have a deep passion for all things .NET, especially Blazor. But I also enjoy learning new technologies and languages. In my last two roles, I have had opportunities to not only use my skills in .NET, but also jump in and get hands-on experience with other technologies such as PHP, React, Microservices, and more.

Outside of programming and technology, my passion for learning plays a key role in my life. When I'm not coding, I enjoy reading; studying maths and science; and taking online classes from universities around the globe.
